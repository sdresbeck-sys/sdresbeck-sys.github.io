About

I design, build and run multi model AI systems from end to end. I choose models by benchmark, chain them into reliable pipelines, score every output automatically, and ship to real users with tests, guardrails and known costs.

Most recently I designed and built MyFamilyStory, a consumer web app with 16 AI products. They turn old family photos and records into restored portraits, narrated videos, researched historical scenes, custom voices and live conversations. I built the pipelines, the front end, the backend jobs, the release process and the product films.

Before that, I built an AI calling system on my own that replaced a manual cold caller at a real estate company, calling every new lead in under a minute.
